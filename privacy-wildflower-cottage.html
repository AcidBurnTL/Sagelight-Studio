Wildflower Cottage
Privacy Policy

This policy covers only the iOS game Wildflower Cottage. It does not replace the studio privacy policy for SageBills, SageMeet, and SageDocs.

Last updated: 21 November 2026
In brief: Wildflower Cottage is an iOS game from Sagelight Studio. The game's own code does not collect personal data. Ads are shown through Google AdMob. Every ad, including rewarded ads, is requested as non-personalised. The game does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, does not access the IDFA, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform may appear, because even non-personalised ads can use device identifiers or similar storage for frequency capping, aggregated reporting, and fraud prevention. This policy is effective from the game's first App Store release.

01 Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is handled when you use Wildflower Cottage, an iOS game published by Sagelight Studio, including ads shown in the game through Google AdMob.

It applies only to Wildflower Cottage. The studio's other apps — SageBills, SageMeet, and SageDocs — are covered by the separate Sagelight Studio Privacy Policy and GDPR Disclosure. Those documents describe local-first apps that do not contain advertising. They do not describe this game.

This policy is written to meet the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), the ePrivacy Directive where it applies, the UK GDPR, Swiss data-protection rules as they affect consent for ads, and Apple's App Store privacy requirements.

This policy is effective from the game's first App Store release.

02 Who we are

Sagelight Studio is the trading name under which Florin Nica, an independent individual developer based in Romania, European Union, publishes this game. For the GDPR (Article 4(7)), Florin Nica is the data controller for Wildflower Cottage, to the extent the game determines the purposes and means of processing (including the decision to show AdMob ads, to request those ads as non-personalised, and to present UMP messages).

  • Data controller: Florin Nica (natural person)
  • Trading as: Sagelight Studio
  • Legal status: Independent individual developer; no separate legal entity (no PFA, no SRL, no other registered form)
  • Location: Romania, European Union
  • Verified Apple developer of record: apps.apple.com/developer/florin-nica/id1889684772
  • Contact (general and data-subject requests): contact@sagelight-studio.com

Google's role. Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK and UMP) is provided by Google. Across AdMob, the publisher and Google each operate as independent controllers of personal data: Google regularly decides how to deliver and improve the ads product, and its use of data remains governed by its contract with publishers and by the settings the publisher chooses. For EEA users, the relevant Google entity is typically Google Ireland Limited; Google LLC (United States) may process data as a Google affiliate. We do not treat Google as our processor for AdMob ads personalisation or ad serving under the controller–controller terms Google publishes for AdMob.

How Google uses information received from apps that use its services is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

03 What the game itself collects

The game's own code does not collect personal data. In particular, Wildflower Cottage does not collect:

  • Contact information (name, email address, phone number, physical address, or other contact details)
  • User content (gameplay content, photos, messages, or other user-generated content sent to us)
  • Account or registration data — there is no Sagelight Studio account for this game
  • Precise location
  • Health, financial, browsing-history, search-history, or sensitive data
  • Data for tracking, as Apple defines that term

Sagelight Studio does not operate a game server that receives your personal data, and the game does not send personal data to us.

Any gameplay progress or settings that remain only on your device, and are never transmitted off the device, are not “collected” in the sense used in Apple's App Store privacy labels.

Purchases are handled by Apple (see Section 8).

04 Data collected by Google AdMob

The only SDKs in the game that collect data are:

  • the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), which requests and displays ads, including rewarded ads; and
  • Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), which can present a consent / privacy message, in particular to users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland.

These SDKs may send data to Google. According to the App Store Connect privacy answers for Wildflower Cottage 1.0, and consistent with Google's App Store data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK (practices described as of SDK 13.12.0), the following data types may be collected:

Linked to the user

  • Coarse location. This can include location derived from IP address, or from Apple's Approximate Location Services API if the user has allowed it. The game does not collect precise location. Google's disclosure states that coarse-location collection through certain SDK controls can be disabled. This policy does not state that those controls have been used.
  • Device ID. Google describes this as a device advertising identifier or other app- or developer-bounded device identifiers, which may be used for third-party advertising and analytics. In this game there is no ATT prompt, so the IDFA is not available and is not used. Other non-IDFA identifiers that the SDK may use can still fall under this category.
  • Product interaction. For example, app-launch taps and interaction information such as video views, which Google says may be used to improve advertising performance.
  • Advertising data. For example, advertisements the user has seen, which Google says may be used for analytics and advertising features.

Not linked to the user

  • Crash data (diagnostics). Google describes non-user-related crash logs, which may be used to diagnose problems and improve the SDK, and may also be used for advertising and analytics.
  • Performance data. Google describes user-associated performance data such as app launch time, hang rate, or energy usage, which may be used to evaluate behaviour, understand product features, and may also be used for displaying ads, including sharing with other entities that display ads.
  • Other diagnostic data.

The game does not use this data for tracking (Apple's term for linking data from this app with data from other companies' apps or websites for targeted advertising or advertising measurement, or sharing it with a data broker).

Optional AdMob features or tests can involve additional data. This policy reflects the 1.0 answers and Google's standard Mobile Ads SDK disclosure. If later versions enable extra features, this policy and the App Store labels will be updated.

Google may also receive standard technical information that accompanies a network request, such as IP address. Google states that it may use IP address to identify a general location, to measure the effectiveness of ads, and, depending on settings, to improve ad relevance. In this game, ad requests are flagged as non-personalised, so Google should not use the data to build or apply an advertising profile for personalised ads (see Section 7).

For a fuller picture of Google's processing, see Google's partner-sites notice, Google's advertising page, and the Google Privacy Policy.

05 Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, the bases are as follows.

Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, and the ePrivacy rules on access to device storage). In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, Google's EU User Consent Policy requires publishers to obtain legally valid consent to (1) the use of cookies or other local storage where legally required, and (2) the collection, sharing, and use of personal data for personalisation of ads. This game requests only non-personalised ads, so it does not seek consent in order to show personalised ads. Consent may still be required for cookies or mobile advertising identifiers used even with non-personalised ads — Google states that those identifiers are still used for frequency capping and aggregated ad reporting, and that consent for those purposes must be obtained where legally required (for example in the UK, Switzerland, and certain EEA countries). UMP is included in the game to present that message and record the choice.

Google's own bases. When a site or app collects consent and passes it to Google, Google says it will use the data for the purposes described in that consent. Google's own privacy policy also describes other grounds it may rely on, including legitimate interests, for purposes such as providing, maintaining and improving its services, measuring advertising, and protecting against fraud and abuse. Those grounds are Google's, as an independent controller. They are described in the Google Privacy Policy and the partner-sites notice.

Apple in-app purchases. Payments are processed by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy (see Section 8).

If you refuse or withdraw the consent UMP asks for, the game can still be used. Ads may be limited or unavailable, including rewarded ads, depending on Google's ad-serving rules and on whether ads can be requested after the consent check.

06 Consent, UMP, and how to change or withdraw it

The game includes Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. Under Google's guidance, consent information is updated on launch. If a message is required, UMP can present it before ads are requested. Ads are requested only when UMP reports that ads may be requested (canRequestAds), which can include a choice already stored from a previous session.

UMP messages are configured in the AdMob Privacy & messaging tools. For personalised ads in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, Google requires a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF). This game requests non-personalised ads; UMP is still shipped so that required disclosures and storage/identifier consent can be handled in those regions.

How to change or withdraw consent

  • Use Privacy settings / Privacy options in the game's settings menu, when that entry is shown. Google requires a visible entry point when UMP reports that a privacy-options form is required, so that you can change or withdraw your choice at any time.
  • You can also email contact@sagelight-studio.com.
  • Uninstalling the game removes the game and locally stored consent state from that device.
  • Independently of the game, you can review or limit ads personalisation in Google's Ad Settings / My Ad Center and in the device Settings related to Apple's advertising identifier. Because this game does not request ATT permission, iOS will not show an App Tracking prompt for it, and the IDFA is not available to the game.

Google's EU User Consent Policy also requires publishers to retain records of consent and to give clear instructions for revocation. UMP is intended to hold that record on the device / through Google's consent tools; Sagelight Studio does not keep a separate server-side copy of your consent choice.

07 Non-personalised ads and why there is no ATT prompt

Every ad request from Wildflower Cottage, including rewarded ads, is sent as non-personalised (npa=1).

Google describes non-personalised ads as ads that are not based on a user's past behaviour. They may use contextual information, including coarse geo-targeting (for example city-level) based on current location, and the content of the current app. Google disallows data used for personalisation, including demographic targeting and user-list targeting, when serving non-personalised ads.

Non-personalised ads do not use cookies or mobile ad identifiers for ad targeting. They do still use cookies or mobile ad identifiers for frequency capping and aggregated ad reporting. They may also be used in connection with fraud and invalid-traffic prevention, and Google may still use IP address for very coarse geo-targeting.

Because the game does not track users and does not need the IDFA, it does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt. Without that authorisation, the IDFA is not available. That is intentional for version 1.0, not an omission in this policy.

You may still see ads. They should be contextual or otherwise non-personalised, not selected from a profile built from your activity in other apps. If you withhold storage/identifier consent where UMP requires it, Google may serve a more limited form of advertising, or ads may not be available.

08 In-app purchases

In-app purchases in Wildflower Cottage are processed by Apple through the App Store. Sagelight Studio does not receive your payment card number or other payment-instrument details. Apple's handling of payment and account data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

The game itself does not collect contact information or purchase records as personal data sent to Sagelight Studio.

09 International transfers

Sagelight Studio is based in Romania (EU) and does not itself receive or store the advertising data described in Section 4.

Google may process AdMob and UMP data in the United States and in other countries. Google publishes controller-to-controller data protection terms for AdMob, which include Standard Contractual Clauses where they apply, and describes its approach to international transfers in its business GDPR documentation. Google LLC also participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which the studio already cites for the Google Ads tag on sagelight-studio.com.

For details of Google's transfers, safeguards, and retention, see the Google Privacy Policy, How Google retains data, and business.safety.google/gdpr.

10 Retention

Sagelight Studio. We do not receive a copy of the AdMob data listed in Section 4, so we do not retain it. If you email us (for example to exercise a right), we keep that correspondence only as long as needed to respond and to meet legal obligations, then delete it. Consent records are stored by UMP on the device / in Google's consent tools, not on a Sagelight Studio server.

Google. Google decides how long it keeps advertising data, as an independent controller. Google states, among other things, that it anonymises advertising data in server logs by removing part of the IP address after 9 months and cookie information after 18 months, and that some data may be kept longer for limited purposes such as security and fraud prevention. See How Google retains data we collect and Advertising.

On your device. Uninstalling Wildflower Cottage removes the app and the data it stored locally on that device, including local consent state. It does not by itself delete data Google may already have received.

12 Children

Wildflower Cottage is a relaxing general-audience game intended for adults. It is not directed at children and has no specific age restriction beyond the App Store age rating. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under 16 in the EEA where that is the age of digital consent (for example, Romania).

The game itself collects no personal data, and all ads are requested as non-personalised without the IDFA. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided data, contact contact@sagelight-studio.com and we will help with Google's tools and delete what we can.
