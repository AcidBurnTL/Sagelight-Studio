Is iCloud or Google Drive safe for your passport and ID?

Both iCloud and Google Drive encrypt everything you upload, so in one sense the answer is "yes, they're safe". For identity documents, though, the more useful question is: could someone other than you decrypt the file? By default, on both services, the answer is yes. Here's how each one works, what you can change, and what we'd recommend for passport and ID scans.

The short answer

  • iCloud with default settings: files in iCloud Drive and Photos are encrypted, but Apple holds the keys.
  • iCloud with Advanced Data Protection: iCloud Drive, Photos, backups and more become end-to-end encrypted. Only your devices hold the keys. This is the strongest option either service offers personal users.
  • Google Drive (personal account): encrypted in transit and at rest, with keys managed by Google. Client-side encryption exists, but only for organisations on Google Workspace whose admin enables it.

How iCloud encrypts your files by default

Apple's default is called Standard Data Protection. Under it, 15 categories are end-to-end encrypted, including Passwords and Keychain, Health data and Messages in iCloud. But iCloud Drive, Photos, iCloud Backup and Notes are encrypted with keys Apple stores, which lets Apple decrypt them "on your behalf" when needed, for example to help you recover your account (Apple).

So a passport scan in iCloud Drive or your photo library is protected from strangers on the internet, but not from Apple itself, or from anyone who can compel Apple to hand data over.

What Advanced Data Protection changes

Advanced Data Protection (ADP) is Apple's optional setting that moves 10 more categories to end-to-end encryption: iCloud Backup, iCloud Drive, Photos, Notes, Reminders, Safari Bookmarks, Shortcuts, Voice Memos, Wallet passes and Freeform. That brings the total to 25 end-to-end encrypted categories (Apple).

The trade-offs are worth knowing before you switch it on:

  • Recovery is your job. You must set up a recovery contact or a recovery key first. If you lose access, Apple can't recover the data for you.
  • All your devices need recent software. ADP requires every device on the account to run a version that supports it.
  • Some things stay outside it. iCloud Mail, Contacts and Calendars are not end-to-end encrypted, and items shared with "anyone with the link" fall back to standard protection.
  • Sharing needs everyone on board. Shared folders and albums stay end-to-end encrypted only if every participant has ADP turned on.
  • iCloud.com web access is off by default once ADP is enabled.

One regional exception: since early 2025, Apple can no longer offer ADP to new users in the United Kingdom, and existing UK users have been asked to turn it off; ADP remains available in all other regions (Apple).

How Google Drive encrypts your files

Google's Drive Help says files you upload are "encrypted in transit and at rest with AES256 bit encryption" (Google Drive Help). Google's documentation on default encryption is clear about the keys: "We own and manage the keys used in default encryption at rest" (Google Cloud).

Google also offers client-side encryption, where Google can't read the files, but it is a Google Workspace feature that your organisation's administrator has to enable (Google Drive Help). A personal Google account doesn't have an equivalent of Apple's ADP.

So where should a passport scan live?

For most people with an iPhone, there are two good options:

  1. Turn on Advanced Data Protection and keep the scans in iCloud Drive. Set up the recovery contact or key carefully first.
  2. Encrypt the document before it reaches any cloud. An app that encrypts on your device and only syncs the encrypted file gives you end-to-end protection regardless of your iCloud settings, and also works for UK users who can't enable ADP.

Whichever you choose, avoid public sharing links for identity documents, use two-factor authentication, and keep scans out of your photo library. Our guide on storing passport and ID scans safely covers the everyday habits that matter just as much as encryption.

FAQ

Can Apple see my files in iCloud Drive?

With the default Standard Data Protection, Apple holds the keys for iCloud Drive and can decrypt files on your behalf. With Advanced Data Protection on, only your trusted devices hold the keys.

Is Google Drive end-to-end encrypted?

Not for personal accounts. Files are encrypted in transit and at rest, but Google manages the keys. Client-side encryption is available only through Google Workspace when an administrator enables it.

Does Advanced Data Protection cover photos?

Yes. Photos is one of the 10 categories that become end-to-end encrypted when you turn on Advanced Data Protection.
