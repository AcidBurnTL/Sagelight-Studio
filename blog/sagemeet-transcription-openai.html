Meeting transcription on iPhone: what stays on the phone and what goes to OpenAI

In short: in SageMeet, the meeting is recorded and kept on the iPhone. Transcription, the summary and search by meaning use OpenAI, though. For those, audio or text goes to OpenAI, with your own API key, only when you run an AI feature. Nothing goes to Sagelight. If you want nothing to leave the phone, you turn on Private Mode. Below you will find which data goes where and what OpenAI says it does with it.

A recorded meeting can contain things you would not say in public: figures, names, decisions, sometimes personal problems. That is why it is worth knowing exactly which way each piece goes.

What stays on the iPhone

The recording itself is local. SageMeet saves the sound on the iPhone, as an M4A file, with no streaming and no upload.

Also on the phone:

  • the recordings, the transcripts and the generated notes;
  • the index for search by meaning, in a local SQLite database;
  • your OpenAI key, in the iOS Keychain.

SageMeet also reads the calendar, so it knows which meeting it is about. Access is read-only: the app does not change anything in the calendar.

Sagelight Studio has no server for SageMeet. We do not receive recordings, transcripts or your key. The app collects neither usage statistics nor error reports.

What goes to OpenAI, and when

The AI features in SageMeet run on OpenAI’s servers, not on the phone. The privacy policy lists all of them:

  • Transcription. The audio goes to Whisper, OpenAI’s transcription service, and the text comes back to the iPhone.
  • The summary. The meeting text goes to GPT-4o, which writes the summary, the decisions and the action items.
  • Search by meaning. The text is sent so it can be turned into vectors. The vectors are then kept locally.
  • Speaker analysis. Speaking time and participation are calculated through GPT-4o-mini.

The rule on the SageMeet page is simple: audio and text go to OpenAI only when you run an AI feature. Before you use the AI features, the app asks for your consent to processing through OpenAI.

If you do not want the audio of a particular meeting to reach OpenAI, turn on Private Mode before you record.

The data travels over the internet. OpenAI’s API infrastructure is in the United States, so a transcription means a data transfer outside the EU.

Your key, your account

SageMeet uses the BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) model: you bring your own OpenAI API key. Requests go straight from the iPhone to OpenAI, authenticated with your key. The key stays in the iOS Keychain and does not reach us.

That has two practical consequences:

  • The cost. The app is free. You pay OpenAI directly for what you use, with no subscription to us. See the usage in your OpenAI account.
  • The relationship with OpenAI. You use the OpenAI API through your own account, not through an account of ours. OpenAI’s rules for the API apply to the data that is sent.

You enter the key in the app before you use the AI features.

What OpenAI does with data sent through the API

We read OpenAI’s documentation, not only what we say on the site. In short:

  • Model training. OpenAI writes that, since 1 March 2023, data sent through the API is not used to train or improve models, unless you explicitly choose to share it.
  • Temporary retention. OpenAI may keep data sent through the API for up to 30 days, in order to detect abuse. After that it deletes it, unless the law requires it to keep the data longer. In the table in the documentation, audio transcription appears with no retention period. For text, meaning summaries and search, the 30-day limit applies.
  • Who has access. Access to this data is limited to authorized employees and to contractors bound by confidentiality, for technical support, investigating abuse and legal obligations.
  • The transfer out of the EU. OpenAI’s data processing agreement is based on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission, or on adequacy decisions of the Commission.

OpenAI’s rules can change. The links to the official pages are at the end.

Private Mode: local recording only

Private Mode is a single toggle that stops every external call. With it on, SageMeet becomes a local voice recorder. You record, listen and organize the meetings, but nothing goes to OpenAI or anywhere else.

What you lose: transcription, the summaries and search by meaning, because all of them depend on OpenAI.

You turn the toggle on from the app.

It is a good choice for a meeting you want to keep as sound only.

iCloud is not OpenAI

SageMeet can optionally sync the meetings through iCloud, so you have them on your other Apple devices. That is a separate path, between the phone and your iCloud account, managed by Apple. The data is encrypted by Apple in transit and at rest.

If you do not want the meetings to reach iCloud either, keep iCloud sync off.

Before a sensitive meeting

A few simple habits:

  • Tell the people in the meeting that you are recording and, if it applies, that the transcription goes through OpenAI.
  • Decide beforehand whether the meeting needs a transcription. If it does not, turn on Private Mode.
  • Check the summary. It is generated automatically. Names, figures and deadlines are worth checking in the transcript or in the recording.
  • Delete what you no longer use. You can delete a recording, or all the data, at any time, from Settings.
  • Use a dedicated API key and follow its usage in the OpenAI account.

SageMeet is free on the App Store. The details are on the SageMeet page, and what each app keeps is explained in the privacy policy.
