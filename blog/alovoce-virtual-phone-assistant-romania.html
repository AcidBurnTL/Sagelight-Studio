AloVoce: a virtual phone assistant for businesses in Romania

A clinic, a garage or a guesthouse loses the call that rings while someone is already with a customer. AloVoce is a separate Romanian product that puts a virtual assistant, Ana, on that phone line. She speaks Romanian, takes appointments and leaves a short summary. It is not one of my apps.

What Sagelight is, and what AloVoce is

I build Sagelight Studio: local-first iPhone and Mac apps. SageDocs keeps documents, SageBills tracks utility bills, SageMeet records meetings. None of them answers a landline.

AloVoce is someone else’s product. I don’t own it, I don’t hold equity in it, and this note is not a paid placement. I recommend it because a lot of small businesses in Romania still miss calls, and the public description matches that problem. The details below are what alovoce.ai said on 6 November 2026. Check their site before you rely on a feature.

What Ana does

Their line is: “Când nu răspunde nimeni, răspunde Ana.” When nobody answers, Ana does. The site describes her as a virtual assistant who picks up the phone, speaks naturally in Romanian, notes the appointments and leaves a two-line summary after each call.

Când nu răspunde nimeni, răspunde Ana.

The summary is the practical part. You don’t have to replay a recording to learn who called and what they wanted. Two lines, after the call.

Who it is for

On that date the site marked six verticals as available:

  • Dental clinics
  • Auto service
  • Beauty salons and barbers
  • Veterinary clinics
  • Physiotherapy and recovery clinics
  • Guesthouses and hotels

Those are businesses where one or two people at the desk cannot pick up while they are with a patient, a car on the lift, or a guest at the counter. A missed call is often a missed booking.

Appointments, confirmation and the calendar

Bookings can go through with or without a person in the loop. Ana can confirm the appointment on the spot, or leave it for you to confirm. You choose in Settings, and you can change it later.

If the business uses Google Calendar, Ana can work with it: she does not offer slots that are already taken, and she writes the appointments into the calendar.

Guesthouses and hotels are the exception they state outright. There, you always confirm the reservation yourself. Ana does not close a hotel booking on her own.

Why I am linking to it

A voicemail that nobody listens to in the evening is not a receptionist. A reply in Romanian, plus a two-line summary, is closer to what a small team actually needs when the phone rings at the wrong moment. That is the whole recommendation.

Not a Sagelight app. AloVoce answers the phone. SageDocs, SageBills and SageMeet stay on the device. If the fit is right, the product is at alovoce.ai. It is also listed on the partners page.

Questions

Is AloVoce part of Sagelight Studio?

No. Sagelight is my iPhone and Mac software. AloVoce is a separate virtual phone assistant. This article is a recommendation, not a sponsorship and not a claim that I own the product.

What happens after a call?

According to AloVoce, Ana leaves a two-line summary of the call. She can also record the appointment, either confirming it herself or leaving it for a person to confirm.

Can a hotel let Ana confirm a room?

No. AloVoce says that for guesthouses and hotels the owner always confirms the reservation.
