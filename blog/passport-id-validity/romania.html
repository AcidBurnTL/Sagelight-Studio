Romania: passport and ID card validity, fees and renewal

A Romanian adult passport (pașaport simplu electronic) is valid for 10 years and costs 265 lei. The electronic identity card (Cartea Electronică de Identitate, CEI) is valid for 10 years for adults and has no expiry date from age 70. Both are applied for in person, by online appointment through the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MAI) HUB.

What to bring

  • Valid identity document (ID card, provisional ID or old ID booklet), original
  • Proof of payment (or the electronic payment record with transaction number)
  • Your previous passport, if you have one
  • For minors: birth certificate and the consent of both parents (notarised consent if one parent cannot attend)

Summary of the official checklist (Direcția Generală de Pașapoarte); requirements differ for special cases, so check before your appointment.

Worth knowing: Old 1997-model identity cards can be swapped for a CEI at any time, even before they expire.

Before you travel

Inside the EU and Schengen area you can travel with either a valid passport or a national ID card, as long as it is valid on the day of travel. Outside the EU, check the destination’s entry rules: some countries require your passport to remain valid for months after you arrive (Your Europe). Setting a reminder well ahead of the expiry date is the simplest way to avoid a rushed renewal; see how to get reminded before your passport or ID card expires.
