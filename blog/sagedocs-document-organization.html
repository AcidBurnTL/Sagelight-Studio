How to organize your documents in SageDocs: categories, issuer and notes

In short: a document is easy to find when all of your documents are filled in by the same rule. In SageDocs, each document has a title, a category, an issuer and notes, plus the reference number and the document dates. Choose one formula for the title, put each document in a single category, write the issuer exactly as on the paper, and use the notes for what does not fit in the other fields. Below is the rule, step by step, and examples for the ordinary documents people keep in Romania.

The problem is usually not that a document is missing, but that it exists under three different names. “Buletin”, “CI Andrei” and “act identitate nou” can be the same document or three different documents, and a year later you no longer know which is which. The fields are the same for every document. The difference is how consistently you fill them in.

The title: one formula, applied to every document

The title is what you see first, so it should say at a glance what the document is and whose it is. A formula that works for almost anything:

[Document type] – [Holder or subject] – [Detail that sets it apart]

  • Carte de identitate – Andrei (Romanian ID card)
  • Pașaport – Maria (passport)
  • Poliță RCA – Dacia Logan – 2026 (compulsory motor insurance)
  • Contract închiriere – Str. Florilor 12, ap. 4 (rental contract)

A few small rules that save you a lot of trouble:

  • Use the same words every time. If you chose “Carte de identitate”, do not write “Buletin” on the next document. Both name the Romanian ID card. Pick the form you naturally search for, and stick to it.
  • Add the holder when the household has more than one document of the same kind. Two passports named only “Pașaport” are a guessing game.
  • Add the year only where there are successive versions. An RCA policy (poliță RCA) is renewed, so “– 2026” helps you tell them apart. For the ID card (buletin) you do not need the year in the title.
  • Do not put the series and number in the title. SageDocs has a separate field for the reference number, and the title stays short and easy to read.

The category: one logical place for each document

SageDocs comes with ready-made categories. The privacy policy gives examples such as identity cards, insurance, loans and contracts. The practical rule: choose the category by what the document is, not by the subject it belongs to.

The car’s RCA policy (poliță RCA) is insurance, even though it belongs to the car. The credit contract for the car belongs with loans. The rental contract (contract de închiriere) is a contract, not a “home” document. If you wonder, for each document, where you would put it, decide once, write the rule down, and apply it the same way every time.

The issuer: who issued the document, written exactly as on the paper

The issuer is the organization that issued the document. It is the field many people skip, but it is the one that makes the difference when you have similar documents from different institutions.

  • The buletin (carte de identitate, the Romanian ID card) is issued by the community public service for population records where you filed the application. On the document it usually appears as an abbreviation (for example SPCLEP), followed by the locality or the sector. Copy exactly what is written on the document.
  • The passport: enter the authority written in the passport, as it appears there.
  • The RCA policy (poliță RCA): the issuer is the insurance company that issued the policy. The broker, or the site you bought it from, is not the issuer. If you want to remember that, note it in the notes.
  • The rental contract (contract de închiriere) has no issuing institution. A useful convention is to enter the other party: the owner or the agency.

Consistency matters here too: write the issuer the same way everywhere, not once with the abbreviation on the document and another time with the full name or in lowercase. The same for insurers: always use the same form of the name.

Notes: what does not fit in the other fields

Notes are free text, so it is tempting to throw anything in there. They work better if you use them for the information you actually look for when the document is in front of you:

  • where the original is: “Originalul în dosarul albastru, sertarul de sus” (“The original is in the blue folder, the top drawer”);
  • who the contact is: the insurance agent, the owner, with a phone number;
  • what the document covers, briefly: “RCA, fără CASCO” (“RCA, without CASCO”);
  • the contract details you forget: “Preaviz: câte zile scrie în contract, art. X” (“Notice: however many days the contract states, art. X”).

What you do not put in the notes: PINs, passwords, card codes or the answers to security questions. A copy of a document is a problem if it reaches someone who should not have it. Next to your banking details it is a much bigger problem. I wrote about this at more length in the guide on where you keep scans of the ID card and passport.

Three filled-in examples

Carte de identitate (Romanian ID card)

  • Title: Carte de identitate – Andrei
  • Category: the one for identity documents
  • Issuer: exactly as on the document (for example SPCLEP + locality)
  • Notes: “Originalul în portofel. Copie cerută la bancă în martie.” (“The original is in the wallet. A copy was requested at the bank in March.”)

Poliță RCA (compulsory motor insurance)

  • Title: Poliță RCA – Dacia Logan – 2026
  • Category: the one for insurance
  • Issuer: the insurance company on the policy
  • Notes: “Cumpărată prin broker [nume]. Fără CASCO.” (“Bought through broker [name]. No CASCO.”)

Contract de închiriere (rental contract)

  • Title: Contract închiriere – Str. Florilor 12, ap. 4
  • Category: the one for contracts
  • Issuer: the owner (name)
  • Notes: “Preaviz conform art. X. Garanția: o chirie, se returnează la predare.” (“Notice as in art. X. The deposit: one month’s rent, returned when you hand the place over.”)

How to start without ruining your weekend

You do not need to move the whole archive in one evening. Pick the five documents you look for most often, fill them in by the rule above, and only then add the rest. If you realize along the way that a formula does not suit you, change it now, while you have few documents. Later it is much harder.
