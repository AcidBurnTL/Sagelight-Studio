The Hidden Cost of Building Maintenance Fees in Romania

If you live in a Romanian apartment block, you pay a monthly întreținere: your share of the building’s common costs, posted on the noticeboard as a listă de plată. Most residents only look at the total. The law, however, gives every owner the right to see how that total was built, to get copies of the invoices behind it, and to contest it. Here is what the list contains, how each share is calculated, and what to do when a number does not add up.

What întreținere actually covers

Law 196/2018, which governs owners’ associations (asociații de proprietari), sorts the association’s expenses into categories (art. 75). The ones you will see on almost every list are:

  • Per-person costs (art. 82): water and sewage, fuel for hot water, electricity for shared installations, and waste collection.
  • Individual consumption (art. 83): what is read from the meters or heat-cost allocators in your flat, such as cold water, hot water and heating.
  • Costs split by ownership share (art. 84): costs tied to the common property, divided in proportion to your cotă-parte indiviză.
  • The working fund and the repair fund (art. 72 and art. 71), covered below.

Not part of întreținere: the mandatory home insurance policy (PAD, under Law 260/2008) is each owner’s own obligation and is taken out individually, not through the association. If an “insurance” line appears on your list without a general-assembly decision behind it, ask what it is.

How each share is calculated

Every category has its own split key. The one people misunderstand most is the ownership share: the ratio between your flat’s usable floor area and the total usable area of all the flats in the building (art. 2 letter l).

Illustrative example: if your flat has 50 m² of usable area and all the flats together have 2,500 m², your share is 2%. Of a 3,000 lei monthly cost split by ownership share, your part is 60 lei. Per-person costs follow the number of people declared for your flat, and consumption costs follow your own readings. Once you know the key, you can redo the calculation for any line yourself.

What the administrator must do every month

The administrator prepares the payment list, has it checked by the auditor (cenzor) and approved by the executive committee, and posts it within 5 days of receiving the last supplier invoice (art. 66 para. 1 letter m). The administrator must also make the supporting documents available to owners on request (art. 66 para. 1 letter k) and post the association’s monthly income and expenses (art. 66 para. 1 letter o).

Your rights as an owner

  • Documents: you can ask in writing for copies of any association document and pay only the copying cost (art. 28 para. 1).
  • Explanations: you can ask how your contribution was calculated (art. 28 para. 3).
  • Contesting the list: a written complaint to the association within 10 days of the list being posted. The president must reply in writing within 10 days of receiving it (art. 28 para. 3).
  • Next step: if the issue is not resolved within 10 days, you can turn to the town hall department that oversees owners’ associations (art. 28 para. 4 and art. 10).

Keep a registered copy of your complaint. The 10 days run from the date the list is posted, not from when you noticed the problem.

Payment deadline and late penalties

You have at most 30 calendar days from the posting of the list to pay (art. 77 para. 2). Late penalties are set by the general assembly, may not exceed 0.2% per day of delay, apply only after 30 days past the due date, and may not exceed the amount they are applied to (art. 77 para. 1).

The repair fund and the working fund

The working fund (fond de rulment) covers one month of the association’s running costs and is paid in advance, as decided by the general assembly (art. 72). The repair fund (fond de reparații) must be approved every year by the general assembly, based on an amount justified by the executive committee, and may be used only for structural consolidation, thermal rehabilitation and work on the common property (art. 71 para. 2).

Using these funds for anything else is an offence fined with 4,000 to 9,000 lei (art. 102). If you are paying into a repair fund but no general assembly ever approved it, ask for the minutes of that meeting.

Warning signs on a payment list

  • The list does not show which categories the total was split into, or the invoices it is based on are not available.
  • The total jumps without an explanation on the noticeboard or a general-assembly decision.
  • You are charged per person for more people than actually live in your flat.
  • The administrator refuses to show you the documents. Failing to meet the administrator’s legal obligations can be fined with 2,500 to 5,000 lei (art. 102).

Check your list in half an hour

  1. Photograph the list on the noticeboard on the day it goes up and note the date: the 10 days start then.
  2. Check the number of people and the usable area recorded against your flat.
  3. Compare your meter and allocator readings with the ones on the list.
  4. Put this month’s list next to last month’s and look for lines that grew.
  5. For any difference, ask in writing for an explanation or the invoices, and file a complaint within the deadline if needed.

The hard part is step 4: noticing in time that a number went up. Keeping your întreținere month by month in one place, whether a spreadsheet or an app, makes that obvious.

FAQ

How long do I have to contest the întreținere list?

10 days from the date the payment list is posted. The complaint must be in writing and addressed to the owners’ association, and the president must reply in writing within 10 days (Law 196/2018, art. 28 para. 3).

Can I ask for the invoices behind my întreținere?

Yes. You can request copies of any association document in writing, including invoices, paying only the copying cost (art. 28 para. 1). The administrator must make supporting documents available (art. 66).

What late penalties can the association charge?

Penalties apply only after 30 days past the due date, are capped at 0.2% per day, and cannot exceed the amount owed (art. 77 para. 1). The exact level is set by the general assembly.

Is home insurance included in întreținere?

No. The mandatory PAD home insurance under Law 260/2008 is each owner’s individual obligation, not an association expense.
