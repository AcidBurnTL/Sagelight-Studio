How to get reminded before your passport or ID card expires

Passports and ID cards expire quietly. The date is printed on the document, but most people only look at it when they're packing, which is often too late. This guide covers how much validity you actually need to travel, how early you can renew (with Romania as a worked example), and a few reminder setups that make sure you hear about it in time.

How much validity do you need to travel?

Inside the EU

EU citizens can travel within the EU and Schengen area with either a valid passport or a national ID card, and the document "must be valid on the day of travel". Driving licences, bank cards and tax cards are not travel documents (Your Europe).

The three-month rule for visitors to Schengen

Non-EU travellers entering the Schengen area need a passport that stays valid for at least three months after the date they plan to leave, and that was issued within the previous ten years, under Article 6(1)(a) of the Schengen Borders Code (Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs).

The six-month rule further afield

Many countries outside Europe expect six months of passport validity beyond your stay, and airlines can refuse boarding if you don't meet it, even when the destination itself is more relaxed. Rules vary by country, so check the official entry requirements for each trip (The Points Guy).

In practice: treat a passport as "expiring" about six months before the printed date. That's when it starts to limit where you can go.

How early can you renew? Romania as an example

ID card (carte de identitate): Romanian citizens apply for a new card no more than 180 days and no less than 15 days before the current one expires. Applications are resolved within up to 30 days, extendable by up to 15 days, and missing the deadline can bring a fine of 40 to 80 lei (Economica.net, 2019). Check current details with your local population records office (SPCLEP), as rules and fees change.

Passport: appointments for Romania's simple electronic passport are booked online through the Ministry of Internal Affairs platform (hub.mai.gov.ro). The passport service describes issuing times as very short (DGP), but appointment demand rises before the summer travel season, when passport offices have extended their opening hours to cope (Profit.ro). Booking early is the safest plan.

Other EU countries have their own windows and processing times, so check your national passport or ID authority. The reminder timings below are deliberately generous so they work almost everywhere.

When to set your reminders

  • Passport: a first reminder about 9 months before expiry (so you can renew before the six-month rule bites), and a second at 7 months.
  • ID card: a reminder when your renewal window opens (in Romania, 180 days before expiry) and another a month later.
  • Everyone in the household: add each person's documents separately. Family members' passports and ID cards rarely expire on the same date.

Option 1: Calendar or Reminders on your iPhone

The simplest approach uses what's already on your phone:

  1. Open Reminders (or Calendar) and create an item such as "Renew passport (expires 14 Mar 2028)". Put the actual expiry date in the title so you don't need to look it up.
  2. Set the date to your first reminder point, for example nine months before expiry.
  3. Create a second item for the follow-up date.
  4. Repeat for every passport and ID card in the household.

It works, but it's manual: when you renew, you have to remember to delete the old reminders and add new ones, and the reminder doesn't hold the document itself.

Option 2: Keep the document and its expiry date together

A document vault that tracks expiry dates removes the bookkeeping: scan the passport once, the expiry date is stored with it, and the reminder follows the document. When you renew, you replace the scan and the reminder moves with it. FAQ

Can I travel in the EU with an expired ID card?

The EU's own guidance says your travel document must be valid on the day of travel. Don't rely on an expired card being accepted.

How many months before expiry should I renew my passport?

Aim to renew when about nine months remain. That keeps you clear of six-month validity rules and leaves time for appointments and processing.

When can I renew my Romanian ID card?

No more than 180 days and no less than 15 days before it expires, at your local population records office.
