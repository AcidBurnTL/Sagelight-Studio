For immediate release

SageDocs Launches: a Privacy-First Encrypted Document Vault for European Users, Anchoring Sagelight Studio's Three-App Suite Alongside SageBills and SageMeet

ROMANIA, May 2026 · Sagelight Studio

ROMANIA: Sagelight Studio today announced the general availability of SageDocs, a privacy-first encrypted document vault for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, now the flagship of the studio's three-app suite. SageDocs joins the previously released SageBills (utility bill tracker) and SageMeet (AI meeting recorder) as the studio's first paid product, built around a single architectural commitment: documents are encrypted on the user's device with AES-256-GCM before they ever sync, and the encryption keys never leave the device.

All three apps are available immediately on the App Store. SageDocs is offered as an auto-renewing subscription at $2.99 per month or $24.99 per year via Apple's in-app purchase, with a 7-day free trial through Apple's Introductory Offer. SageBills and SageMeet remain free; SageMeet operates on a Bring-Your-Own-Key model where users supply their own OpenAI API key.

SageDocs: Documents That Even the Studio Cannot Read

SageDocs solves a problem most existing solutions paper over: where to keep the documents people actually need to keep safe (identity cards, passports, rental contracts, insurance policies, medical records, loan agreements) without handing the contents to a notes app, a generic cloud drive, or the Photos library where automatic backups expose them to anyone with the device passcode.

Every attachment in SageDocs is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it touches storage. Encryption keys are derived from the user's master PIN through PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations, exceeding the OWASP 2023 minimum, and a 256-bit per-installation salt held in the iOS Keychain. Authentication is gated by Face ID, Touch ID, or a master PIN, with biometric-protected key storage that invalidates if biometric enrollment changes. iCloud sync via CloudKit uses Apple's private database, but the ciphertext arriving at Apple's servers is already encrypted with the user's personal key. Neither Apple nor Sagelight Studio can decrypt a SageDocs document.

"Most of the apps that ask you to trust them with sensitive documents have a recovery mechanism, which is a polite way of saying they hold a key," said Florin Nica, founder of Sagelight Studio. "SageDocs deliberately doesn't. If you lose your PIN and biometric enrollment, the documents are unrecoverable. That sounds harsh, and it is. It is also the only honest architecture for something that calls itself a vault."

A Three-App Suite, One Architectural Philosophy

SageDocs joins two earlier releases that share the studio's privacy-first foundation:

  • SageBills: a free utility bill tracker for European households. Tracks 40+ bill categories across 27 EU countries with 39 pre-loaded providers, on-device OCR scanning for paper invoices via Apple's Vision framework, and Core ML predictions that run entirely on the device. Available since April 2026.
  • SageMeet: a free AI meeting recorder that pairs with the user's own OpenAI API key. Audio recording is fully local; only AI features (transcription via Whisper, summaries via GPT-4o, semantic search) reach OpenAI, and only when the user explicitly triggers them. A Private Mode toggle disables all external transmission. Available since April 2026.
  • SageDocs: the encrypted document vault described above. Now generally available as a subscription product (May 2026).

All three apps are built exclusively on Apple's first-party frameworks: SwiftData, SwiftUI, Vision, CoreSpotlight, CloudKit, Core ML, CryptoKit. None contains a third-party analytics SDK, advertising identifier, crash reporter, or telemetry framework. The studio has no backend that holds user data for any of the three products.

Pricing Across the Suite

  • SageBills: Free. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no account.
  • SageMeet: Free. Users supply their own OpenAI API key and pay OpenAI directly for usage; Sagelight Studio receives no revenue share.
  • SageDocs: $2.99/month or $24.99/year via Apple's in-app subscription. 7-day free trial. After trial expiry without subscription, the app enters read-only mode: existing documents remain viewable and CloudKit sync continues, but new captures and edits require an active subscription. Users who first installed the app at version 1.0.5 or earlier are permanently grandfathered to free Pro access via StoreKit 2's original-purchase verification.

EU and GDPR Posture

Sagelight Studio operates from Romania, European Union. The data controller for all three apps is Florin Nica, a natural person trading as Sagelight Studio. SageBills and SageDocs are local-first with no Sagelight-operated servers; SageMeet transmits data to OpenAI only on explicit user request, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework with Standard Contractual Clauses as the independent fallback transfer mechanism. The marketing website uses a single Google Ads conversion tag under Google Consent Mode v2 with all signals defaulted to denied. The studio's full GDPR Article 13/14 disclosure is published at sagelight-studio.com/gdpr.html.

04 / Journalist resources

FAQ for journalists.

The questions we get most often, answered on the record.

What is SageDocs?

SageDocs is a privacy-first encrypted document vault for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It is the studio's flagship paid product, designed to hold the documents people most need to keep secure: identity cards, rental contracts, insurance policies, medical records, loan agreements, and any other sensitive personal paperwork. Every attachment is encrypted with AES-256-GCM on the user's device before storage; encryption keys are derived from a master PIN through PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations and never leave the device. Authentication uses Face ID, Touch ID, or PIN, with encrypted iCloud sync via CloudKit so documents are available across all the user's Apple devices.

How is SageDocs priced?

SageDocs is an auto-renewing subscription via Apple's in-app purchase: $2.99 per month or $24.99 per year (approximately 30% savings on the yearly tier). A 7-day free trial is available through Apple's Introductory Offer on the monthly tier. After the trial ends without subscription, the app enters read-only mode: existing documents remain viewable and CloudKit sync continues working, but new captures and edits require an active subscription. Users who first installed SageDocs at version 1.0.5 or earlier are permanently grandfathered to free Pro access. Subscription management is handled by Apple via iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Can Sagelight Studio read documents stored in SageDocs?

No. Documents are encrypted with AES-256-GCM on the user's device before any storage or sync occurs. The encryption key is derived from the user's master PIN via PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations and a 256-bit per-installation salt. The key is stored in the iOS Keychain with biometric protection. Sagelight Studio has no access to the user's PIN, the derived key, or the plaintext documents: not on the device, not in transit, not in iCloud. The architecture deliberately precludes recovery: if the user loses their PIN and their biometric enrollment changes, the documents are unrecoverable.

Does iCloud see the documents?

No. SageDocs uses CloudKit's private database for sync, which means data is associated with the user's personal iCloud account. But more importantly, the documents are already encrypted with the user's personal key before they leave the device. Apple stores ciphertext only; even Apple cannot decrypt a SageDocs document. This is one architectural layer beyond standard iCloud encryption.

What about the other two apps, SageBills and SageMeet?

SageBills is a free utility bill tracker for European households. It ships with 39 pre-loaded providers across 27 EU countries, 40+ bill categories, on-device OCR for paper invoices via Apple's Vision framework, and Core ML cost predictions that run entirely offline. Available since April 2026 on iOS 17+ and macOS 14+.

SageMeet is a free AI meeting recorder for iPhone with a Bring-Your-Own-Key model: users supply their own OpenAI API key, and the app pairs that with Apple's audio frameworks to deliver Whisper transcription and GPT-4o summaries. Recording itself is fully local; AI features run only when the user explicitly triggers them. Private Mode disables all external transmission. Available since April 2026.

Both apps share SageDocs's privacy posture: no third-party SDKs, no analytics, no telemetry, no studio-operated servers holding user data.

Why is SageDocs paid but SageBills and SageMeet free?

SageDocs is the studio's first paid product, deliberately. Subscription revenue keeps the studio sustainable without the alternative most apps quietly choose: data collection, behavioral advertising, or selling analytics access. SageBills is supported directly by the developer for now; SageMeet's costs are passed through to the user via their own OpenAI account. The long-term model is honest software paid for honestly: a useful product, priced fairly, with no hidden monetization. Future pricing changes will be communicated in advance and will not affect users grandfathered into free access.

What data does the studio collect?

From the apps: none. No analytics SDKs, no telemetry, no crash reporters, no advertising identifiers, no usage tracking of any kind in any of the three apps. Sagelight Studio has no backend that holds user data. From the marketing website (sagelight-studio.com): a single Google Ads conversion tag runs under Google Consent Mode v2 with all signals defaulted to denied, transmitting cookieless modeled pings only until a visitor explicitly opts in. The full disclosure is published at sagelight-studio.com/gdpr.html.

Is there an Android version?

Not currently. All three apps are built natively on Apple's first-party frameworks: SwiftData, SwiftUI, Vision, CoreSpotlight, CloudKit, Core ML, CryptoKit. An Android version would require a significant rebuild from the ground up and is not on the near-term roadmap. The studio is deliberately focused on delivering the best possible iOS and macOS experience first.

Who is behind Sagelight Studio?

Sagelight Studio is the trading name of Florin Nica, an independent individual developer based in Romania, European Union. The studio is a one-person operation focused on building privacy-respecting productivity and personal finance tools specifically designed for the European market. The developer's identity has been verified by Apple under the Apple Developer Program. Current apps are SageDocs (encrypted document vault, flagship paid product), SageBills (utility bill tracker), and SageMeet (AI meeting recorder, BYOK). All three share the same privacy-first, zero third-party SDK philosophy.
