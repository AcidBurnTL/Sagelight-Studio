01 Introduction

Welcome to Sagelight Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our applications — SageBills (utility bill tracking), SageMeet (meeting recording and AI transcription), and SageDocs (personal document vault) — on iOS and macOS.

This policy complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the ePrivacy Directive, and Apple App Store Guidelines (sections 5.1.1 and 5.1.2).

02 Who We Are

Sagelight Studio is the trading name under which Florin Nica, an independent individual developer based in Romania, European Union, builds and maintains the apps covered by this policy. For the purposes of the GDPR (Article 4(7)), Florin Nica is the data controller.

  • Data Controller: Florin Nica (natural person)
  • Trading as: Sagelight Studio
  • Legal status: Independent individual developer; no separate legal entity (no PFA, no SRL, no other registered form)
  • Location: Romania, European Union
  • Verified developer of record: apps.apple.com/developer/florin-nica/id1889684772 — Apple has verified the identity of the controller as part of the Apple Developer Program enrollment.
  • Contact: contact@sagelight-studio.com

For the purposes of the GDPR, the developer acts as the data controller. For SageBills, all data processing occurs locally and your data never reaches us. For SageMeet, data is processed locally except when you use AI features, which transmit data to OpenAI (see Sections 9 and 10). For SageDocs, data is stored locally and synced to your private iCloud account; no data is transmitted to Sagelight Studio or any third party other than Apple.

03 Privacy-First Architecture

SageBills

SageBills is designed with a local-only, zero-server architecture:

  • No servers. We do not operate any backend servers that receive, store, or process your data.
  • No cloud sync. Your data is not uploaded to any cloud service by SageBills.
  • No analytics. We do not collect usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry of any kind.
  • No tracking. We do not track your behavior, location, or activity.
  • No third-party SDKs. The app is built entirely with Apple frameworks. Zero external dependencies.

SageMeet

SageMeet stores all data locally and optionally syncs via iCloud. When you use AI-powered features:

  • Audio and text are sent to OpenAI APIs (Whisper for transcription, GPT-4o for notes, embedding APIs for search) when you explicitly request AI processing.
  • Private Mode disables all external data transmission. No data leaves your device.
  • No analytics or tracking. SageMeet does not collect usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry.
  • Your OpenAI API key is stored securely in the device Keychain and only used to authenticate requests you initiate.

SageDocs

SageDocs is a personal document vault with enhanced security for sensitive documents such as identity cards, insurance policies, and loan agreements:

  • AES-256-GCM encryption. All document attachments are encrypted before storage using a key derived via PBKDF2-SHA256 (600,000 iterations).
  • Biometric or PIN authentication. The vault is locked behind Face ID, Touch ID, or a master PIN.
  • iCloud sync. Encrypted documents sync via Apple CloudKit to your private iCloud account. Data is encrypted before it reaches iCloud.
  • No AI features. SageDocs does not use artificial intelligence or send data to any external API.
  • No analytics or tracking. No telemetry, crash reports, or third-party SDKs.
  • Privacy-respecting logging. Security events logged via OSLog never contain PINs, encryption keys, or document content.
In simple terms: SageBills data never leaves your device. SageMeet data stays local unless you use AI features. SageDocs encrypts everything and syncs only encrypted data to your private iCloud.

04 Data We Process

SageBills Data

All of the following data is stored exclusively on your device and in your private iCloud account:

Bill Data (User-Entered)
  • Bill amounts and currency
  • Provider/utility company names
  • Bill categories (electricity, gas, water, internet, etc.)
  • Bill dates (issue date, due date)
  • Notes you add to bills
  • Consumption readings (kWh, cubic meters, etc.)
OCR-Scanned Data

When you scan an invoice, Apple's Vision framework extracts text entirely on your device. This may include bill amounts, provider names, account numbers, and service addresses.

The original image is not retained after scanning unless you choose otherwise.

App Preferences & Keychain
  • Currency and display preferences, notification preferences, savings goals, onboarding and consent status
  • GDPR consent date (Keychain, non-personal)

SageMeet Data

The following data is stored on your device and optionally in your private iCloud account:

Recordings & Transcripts
  • Audio recordings — stored locally and optionally in iCloud Drive
  • Transcripts — text generated from audio via OpenAI Whisper API

Search, Calendar & Analytics

  • Speaker analytics — participation metrics processed via GPT-4o-mini

SageMeet Keychain & Preferences
  • OpenAI API key — stored securely in the device Keychain, used only to authenticate your requests
  • Display settings, recording preferences, AI feature toggles, Private Mode status

SageDocs Data

The following data is stored on your device and in your private iCloud account:

Document Metadata
  • Document titles — names you assign to your documents
  • Issuers — organizations that issued the document
  • Reference numbers — document identification numbers
  • Dates — issue dates, expiry dates
  • Notes — free-text notes you add
  • Categories — identity cards, insurance, loans, contracts, medical, etc.
Encrypted Attachments
  • Document scans, photos, PDFs — encrypted with AES-256-GCM before storage
  • Thumbnails — also encrypted
  • Stored using SwiftData's external storage mechanism
Security & Keychain Data
  • Master PIN hash — salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hash (no plaintext PIN stored)
  • Encryption salt — 256-bit random value for key derivation
  • Biometric enrollment state — detects changes in Face ID/Touch ID enrollment
  • Derived encryption key — biometric-protected Keychain item
  • Lockout state — progressive lockout after failed authentication
  • Onboarding status, notification preferences

07 Data Storage and Security

Local Storage (All Apps)

All data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework with NSFileProtectionComplete encryption:

  • Data is encrypted when your device is locked
  • Data is only accessible when your device is unlocked and authenticated
  • Encryption keys are tied to your device passcode/biometrics

SageDocs Enhanced Security

SageDocs provides additional security beyond standard device encryption:

  • AES-256-GCM: All attachments encrypted with keys derived via PBKDF2-SHA256 (600,000 iterations)
  • Keychain-stored salt: 256-bit random salt with kSecAttrAccessibleWhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly
  • Biometric-protected keys: Encryption key invalidated if biometric enrollment changes
  • Constant-time PIN verification: Prevents timing attacks
  • Progressive lockout: Escalating cooldowns after failed authentication
Your responsibility: You are responsible for device backups. For SageDocs, remember your master PIN — we cannot recover it, as we have no access to your encryption keys.

08 Data Retention

Your data is retained on your device for as long as you keep the app installed and choose to retain it. You have full control:

  • Delete individual records at any time within any app
  • Delete all data using the option in Settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all associated data
  • SageDocs: Reset vault to delete all documents and encryption keys

We do not retain any copy of your data because we never receive it.

09 Third-Party Services

Apple iCloud (SageMeet & SageDocs)

SageMeet and SageDocs optionally sync data via Apple's iCloud (CloudKit / iCloud Drive). Apple acts as a data processor under its own GDPR commitments. iCloud data is encrypted in transit and at rest. For SageDocs, attachments are additionally encrypted with AES-256-GCM before reaching iCloud.

OpenAI (SageMeet only)

When you use AI features in SageMeet, audio and text data are sent to OpenAI LLC for processing. OpenAI acts as a data processor under Article 28 GDPR. The following OpenAI services are used:

  • Whisper API — audio-to-text transcription
  • GPT-4o — meeting note generation and summarization
  • GPT-4o-mini — speaker analytics processing
  • Embedding API — semantic vector generation for search

OpenAI does not use API data to train their models. OpenAI is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (DPF). You authenticate with your own API key; we have no access to your OpenAI account.

SageBills & SageDocs: No Other Third Parties

SageBills and SageDocs contain no third-party SDKs, frameworks, libraries, or services beyond Apple's own frameworks. We do not share, sell, or disclose your data to any third party.

10 International Data Transfers

SageBills does not transfer your data internationally. Your data remains on your device at all times.

SageMeet transfers data to the United States when you use AI features, as OpenAI's API infrastructure is located in the US. These transfers are conducted under two complementary lawful mechanisms: (1) the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (DPF) under Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795, under which OpenAI LLC is a certified participant; and (2) the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) under Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, incorporated into OpenAI's Data Processing Addendum (Art. 46(2)(c)). If the DPF is invalidated, SCCs remain the independent lawful basis. When Private Mode is enabled, no international transfers occur.

SageDocs does not transfer your data to any third party. iCloud sync is managed by Apple under Apple's own GDPR commitments.

11 Cookies and Tracking

In our apps

No Sagelight Studio app (SageBills, SageMeet, SageDocs) uses cookies, web beacons, pixels, device fingerprinting, advertising identifiers, or any other tracking technology. No app contains any advertising.

On this marketing website (sagelight-studio.com)

The marketing website loads the Google Ads conversion tag (AW-17758801825) to measure the effectiveness of paid advertising campaigns that link to the App Store.

The tag is configured under Google Consent Mode v2 (Advanced) with all four signals set to "denied" by default:

  • ad_storage — denied (no advertising cookies stored)
  • ad_user_data — denied (no user data shared with Google for ads)
  • ad_personalization — denied (no personalised advertising)
  • analytics_storage — denied (no analytics cookies; reserved for future use)

Two further protective flags are also set on every page:

  • ads_data_redaction: true — Google redacts identifying information from the cookieless pings sent while consent is denied.
  • url_passthrough: true — when consent is denied, click identifiers (gclid) travel via URL parameters only, never via cookies, so ad attribution survives without device storage.

In practice:

  • No cookies are stored on your device unless you explicitly opt in via the consent banner.
  • While consent is denied, Google receives only redacted, cookieless "pings" containing minimal functional information — timestamp, user agent, source URL, a boolean indicating whether a click identifier was present, a per-pageview random number, and the consent state itself. These pings power Google's modelled (estimated) conversion reporting; no individual is identified.
  • If you opt in via the banner, you can grant Advertising and Analytics consent independently. A Google Ads cookie may then be stored to attribute App Store visits to specific ad campaigns.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in measuring marketing effectiveness without storing identifiers) for the cookieless default-denied state; Article 6(1)(a) GDPR (specific, informed, freely-given consent) for the opted-in state.

Data recipient: Google Ireland Limited (processor, EU establishment) and Google LLC (sub-processor, United States), under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the Standard Contractual Clauses incorporated into Google's Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms.

Withdrawal of consent: at any time via the Cookie preferences link in the footer, by clearing site cookies, by using a browser-level tracking blocker, or by emailing us at contact@sagelight-studio.com. Your choice is persisted only in your browser's localStorage; nothing about it is transmitted to us.

The tag does not run inside any of our apps; it is exclusive to this marketing website.
