03 / Principles

The rules every app ships with.

No investors, no growth team, no engagement metrics. Software made with care for the people who use it.

  1. Local-first by default

    Your data lives on your device. We run no servers that hold your bills, meetings or documents.

  2. GDPR is the floor, not the ceiling

    Designed around Articles 5, 25 and 32: privacy by design, by default, with security in the architecture.

  3. No ads. No accounts. No tracking.

    No third-party SDKs, no analytics, no advertising IDs, no telemetry.

  4. Native Apple frameworks only

    SwiftUI, SwiftData, Vision, CloudKit. Fast, light and idiomatic.

  5. Honest pricing

    SageBills and SageMeet are free. SageDocs is a plain subscription. No dark patterns.

  6. Built by one person, in Romania

    An independent, one-person studio in the EU, identity-verified by Apple.
