Questions

Things people ask first.

How much does SageDocs cost?
SageDocs is offered as an auto-renewing subscription via the App Store at $2.99 per month or $24.99 per year. You can cancel anytime in iPhone Settings → [your Apple ID] → Subscriptions; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.
How is my data encrypted?
Every attachment is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before storage. Encryption keys are derived from your master PIN using PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations and a 256-bit per-installation salt. Keys live in the iOS Keychain and are biometric-protected.
What happens if I forget my master PIN?
Your encrypted documents cannot be recovered. We do not have a backdoor or any way to reset your encryption keys — that is the point of the design. Choose a PIN you can remember, and consider enabling biometric unlock as a backup path.
Does iCloud see my documents?
No. Documents are encrypted on-device with AES-256-GCM before being synced to iCloud via CloudKit. Apple stores ciphertext only — even Apple cannot decrypt your documents.
Can I share a document from SageDocs?
Yes. Documents are temporarily decrypted for the share you initiate (e.g., emailing your insurance card). The temporary file is securely wiped immediately after.
Is SageDocs available for Mac?
Yes. SageDocs is a universal binary running natively on iPhone (iOS 17+), iPad (iPadOS 17+), and Mac (macOS 14+). Documents sync encrypted across all your Apple devices via iCloud.
