Per Article 13(1)(a), Florin Nica, acting in his own name as a natural person under the trading name Sagelight Studio, is the data controller. He personally determines the purposes and means of processing for all Sagelight Studio apps.

Per Article 13(1)(a) and Article 14(1)(a), the developer is the data controller for all Sagelight Studio apps. For SageBills, the controller does not receive, access, or store any personal data. No advertising networks, analytics providers, or other third parties receive data from inside any app. The marketing website (sagelight-studio.com) is treated separately — see below.

Marketing website (sagelight-studio.com)

The marketing website loads the Google Ads conversion tag (AW-17758801825) under Google Consent Mode v2 (Advanced). All four signals are defaulted to "denied"; additionally, the tag is configured with ads_data_redaction: true (Google redacts identifying data from cookieless pings) and url_passthrough: true (click IDs travel via URL parameters, never via cookies, while consent is denied).

Marketing website — Recipients
RecipientRoleData Received
Google Ireland LimitedData processor (Art. 28) — EU establishmentWhile consent is denied: a redacted cookieless ping containing timestamp, user agent, source URL, a boolean indicating whether a click ID is present, a per-pageview random number, and the consent state. No cookies stored, no individual identification. When the visitor opts in: full conversion data including the gclid.
Google LLC (United States)Sub-processor — under EU-US Data Privacy FrameworkSame as above; aggregated for modelled measurement under denial, observed measurement after opt-in.

Consent granularity: the banner lets the visitor toggle Advertising (ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization) and Analytics (analytics_storage) independently. The choice is persisted in browser localStorage only and can be withdrawn at any time via the Cookie preferences link in the footer. No record of the visitor's consent decision is ever transmitted to Sagelight Studio.

This tag is exclusive to the marketing website. It is not present in any Sagelight Studio app.

10 Automated Decision-Making

SageBills uses statistical calculations (averaging, trend analysis) for predictions and anomaly detection. These are informational only and do not constitute profiling or automated decision-making.

SageMeet uses AI models (GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini) to generate meeting notes, summaries, action items, and speaker analytics. These are informational only. Users should review AI outputs for accuracy.

SageDocs uses date-based calculations for expiry tracking and reminders. These are informational only. SageDocs does not use AI or analyze document content.

11 Data Protection Measures

Per Article 32:

All Apps

  • Encryption at rest: NSFileProtectionComplete (data encrypted when device is locked)
  • Privacy by design (Art. 25(1)) and privacy by default (Art. 25(2))

SageBills

  • On-device processing: All OCR and predictions run locally
  • No third-party code: Zero external SDKs

SageMeet

  • User-controlled AI: External transmission only when explicitly requested
  • Private Mode: Complete disabling of all external data transmission
  • Keychain security: Hardware-backed encryption for API keys

SageDocs (Enhanced)

  • AES-256-GCM encryption of all document attachments
  • PBKDF2-SHA256 key derivation (600,000 iterations)
  • 256-bit random salt per installation (Keychain)
  • Biometric-protected key storage (.biometryCurrentSet)
  • Constant-time PIN comparison
  • Progressive lockout after failed authentication
  • Privacy-respecting logging (never contains PINs, keys, or content)
  • Secure temp files with immediate cleanup after sharing
  • No third-party code: Zero external SDKs

12 Breach Notification

Per Articles 33 and 34: Given the local-first architecture, traditional data breaches are not applicable. Should a vulnerability be identified:

  • Users will be notified through an app update
  • ANSPDCP will be notified within 72 hours (Art. 33)
  • Affected data subjects will be notified without undue delay (Art. 34)
