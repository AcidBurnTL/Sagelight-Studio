AloVoce

„Când nu răspunde nimeni, răspunde Ana.”

A virtual phone assistant for businesses in Romania. Ana answers the phone, speaks naturally in Romanian, takes appointments and leaves a two-line summary after each call.

alovoce.ai

On 6 November 2026, alovoce.ai listed these verticals as available:

  • Dental clinics
  • Auto service
  • Beauty salons and barbers
  • Veterinary clinics
  • Physiotherapy and recovery clinics
  • Guesthouses and hotels

Appointments can be confirmed by Ana or left for a person to confirm. That choice lives in Settings and can be changed. If you use Google Calendar, Ana can avoid occupied slots and write the booking into the calendar. For guesthouses and hotels, the owner always confirms the reservation.

AloVoce is not a Sagelight app. SageDocs, SageBills and SageMeet stay on the iPhone and Mac. AloVoce answers the phone. I recommend it as a Romanian product for businesses that miss calls. Read the note on the blog, then check the details on their site.
