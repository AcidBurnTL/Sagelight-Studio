About the author

Florin Nica,one person, three apps.

I'm the independent developer behind Sagelight Studio, based in Romania, EU. I design, build, support and write about every app on this site myself.

What I build

  • SageDocs: an encrypted vault for IDs, passports, contracts and insurance papers, with expiry reminders.
  • SageBills: a utility bill tracker for European households, with on-device invoice scanning.
  • SageMeet: a meeting recorder that transcribes and summarises with your own OpenAI key.

How I work

Every app is built with Apple's own frameworks (SwiftUI, SwiftData, Vision, CloudKit), stores data on your device first, and has no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Sagelight runs no servers that hold your documents, bills or meetings.

Sagelight Studio is my trading name; there is no separate company, no investors and no growth team. My identity is verified by Apple under the Apple Developer Program, and you can see my apps on my App Store developer page.

Why I write the guides

The blog covers the paperwork my apps deal with: passports and ID cards, household bills, Romanian invoices and building fees. I check figures and rules against official sources and list those sources at the end of each article. If something is out of date or wrong, email me and I'll fix it.
